# Leula Events — Design Assets, Typography & Licensing Documentation

This document records the source, creator, and license information for all external fonts, client-side libraries, and bespoke artwork used in the **Leula Events 50-Template Luxury Invitation Collection** and **Invitation Studio (`designer.php` & `pass.php`)**.

---

## 1. Original Vector Artwork & Illustrations (`assets/js/luxe_art.js` & `assets/js/templates_library.js`)

All 50 invitation card illustrations, watercolor washes, gold foil speckles, marble textures, multi-layered floral bouquets, botanical branches, arches, ornamental borders, crests, monograms, and East African heritage motifs (`Kitenge`, `Kanga`, `Swahili Carved Doors`, `Zanzibar Dhow`, `Mudcloth`) are **100% original procedural SVG compositions** authored specifically for the Leula Events platform.

- **Source Files:**
  - `assets/js/luxe_art.js` — Original Procedural Luxury Botanical, Watercolor, Crest & Cultural Vector Engine (`LuxeArt`).
  - `assets/js/templates_library.js` — 50 Bespoke Luxury Stationery Templates (`INVITATION_TEMPLATES_LIBRARY`).
- **Creator:** Leula Events Engineering & Design Studio (Original In-House Procedural SVG Artwork)
- **License:** Proprietary / First-Party Original Work (100% free of third-party copyright restrictions; full commercial usage rights included).
